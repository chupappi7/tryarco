Routines

Your day, set up in one tap.

When you set up , pick up to two routines. Each adds habits with times already set and, for some, Blocked Hours windows.

  • Tap a habit on your timeline to change its time or days, or delete it.
  • Blocked Hours windows live in Control → Blocked Hours.
  • Everything a routine adds is yours to change.

Blocked Hours

Apps shut on a schedule.

Blocked Hours shut the apps you picked during set times, on the days you choose, without you starting anything. Good for work hours, study time or the hour before bed.

  1. Open Control and tap Blocked Hours.
  2. Add a window: a name, start and end time, and days.
  3. Pick how strict it is and how many breaks you get.

It blocks the apps from Settings → Apps to block, and needs Screen Time access.

Focus sessions

Lock in until the timer ends.

A focus session blocks your picked apps until the timer runs out. Start one from Control, or from a timed habit set to block apps, like Deep work or Study.

  • Breaks lift the block for a few minutes, then it comes back by itself.
  • Strict mode can't be ended early. Breaks still work.
  • The session shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
Plan your day

Give everything a time.

Tap Plan your day on Today to place what's waiting: habits without a time, tasks, and quick events like lunch or a meeting. Give each a time and it lands on your timeline.

Planning the night before works best: open it in the evening and pick tomorrow on the date strip.

Habits & streaks

Small things, every day.

A habit can be a simple check, a count (like water ×8) or a timer. Tap one on your timeline to tick it off or start its timer.

  • Your streak grows with every day you keep it.
  • A skipped day doesn't break it.
  • Insights shows your best streaks and how your week went.
Tasks

Everything to do, by project.

The Tasks page on Today holds everything you need to do, grouped by project.

  • Give a task a time and it appears on your timeline.
  • Leave it without one and it waits under Anytime.
  • Mark a task in progress to keep it on top, and tick it off from a widget.
Widgets

Your day, one glance away.

Put your timeline, tasks, habits and screen time on your Home Screen. Tick tasks and habits, and start timers, right from the widget.

Add a widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget, and search for .
  3. Swipe to the widget and size you want, tap Add Widget, then Done.

Choose what it shows

  1. Touch and hold the widget, then tap Edit Widget.
  2. On a habit widget, tap Habit and pick the one to track. On the Tasks widget, tap Show and pick the list.
  3. Tap anywhere outside to save. Want two habits? Add the widget twice and pick a different habit on each.

Wake up

Alarms that start your day.

The Wake up block marks the start of your day on the timeline. Tap it to set real alarms.

  • Pick the time and days, or tap Weekdays or Weekends.
  • Set how many alarms and how far apart: 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  • Choose a sound and snooze, then tap Set alarms.

They ring like the Clock app's alarms, even on silent.
