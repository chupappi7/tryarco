Privacy Policy · Effective 19 October 2026

In short

has no account and no ads. What you plan stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The app sends anonymous usage counts so we can see what helps, keyed to a random ID, never your name. We don't look up your location. Your Screen Time data never leaves your iPhone. We don't sell data.

01Who runs

is an independent app built by Thinh Tran. Contact: support@tryarco.app.

02What you create

Everything you enter (tasks, habits, focus sessions, schedules, blocked apps) is stored on your iPhone. With iCloud on, it also syncs through your own private iCloud database, so on your other devices sees the same plan. That database belongs to your Apple Account: we can't read it. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

collects no name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifier or device identifier.

03Screen Time

uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to block the apps you pick. It holds only opaque tokens for those apps: it doesn't learn which apps you use or what you do in them.

The numbers on Insights are drawn on your iPhone by Apple's own report system, in a sandbox that keeps them away from 's code and from the internet. never sees or sends them: by Apple's design, not just our promise.

04Subscriptions

is a subscription app with a free trial. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never receive your card or billing details. RevenueCat checks App Store receipts to confirm your subscription and receives only a random ID created by the app (RevenueCat's privacy policy).

also saves that random ID in your own iCloud, so an AI agent you connect to can confirm your subscription.

If you ask Apple for a refund, may share information about your use of the app with Apple, through RevenueCat, to help it decide the request: for example how long you've had , how much you've used it and your purchase history. This is described in our Terms, and it never includes your plans, tasks or anything you've typed.

05Notifications and alarms

Reminders, the morning plan, focus updates and wake-up alarms are scheduled and delivered on your device. They never go through a server.

06Anonymous usage data

To learn which parts of help people, the app sends a small amount of usage data to PostHog, an analytics service, on its EU servers (PostHog's privacy policy):

  • Events such as "onboarding finished", "paywall shown" or "focus started", with the app and iOS version.
  • Your answers to onboarding's multiple-choice questions, and which ready-made habits you picked.
  • A daily snapshot of how the app is set up, in rough buckets, like "3–5 habits".
  • Your App Store country, from your App Store account's region. Location lookup from your IP address is turned off.

All of it is keyed to a random ID the app creates on install: not your Apple Account, not an advertising ID, and not linked to your name or plans. Task names, notes and other things you type are never sent, with one exception: if you choose "Other" when asked how you heard about , the few words you type there are sent with it.

has no advertising and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so App Tracking Transparency doesn't apply.

07AI agents (coming soon)

When you connect an AI agent to , the connector runs on your computer. It signs in to your own iCloud with your Apple Account and reads and changes your data there directly: nothing passes through an server. The sign-in is saved only on that computer.

08Crash reports

doesn't collect crash reports. If you share diagnostics with developers in iOS, Apple may pass on anonymized crash logs under its own policy.

09Children

is rated 4+ and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

10Your choices

Your plans live on your devices and in your iCloud, so you control them. Deleting the app removes the local copy; remove the synced copy in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. The anonymous usage data can't be tied back to you, and deleting the app discards the random ID, so nothing sent afterwards joins what came before.

11Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Using after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12Contact
