ARCO vs Opal

Both block distracting apps on iPhone with schedules and focus sessions. The difference is what's around the blocking: Opal is a dedicated blocker, ARCO is a day planner that blocks.

By Thinh Tran, maker of · Updated 20 October 2026

Opal is one of the best-known screen time apps on iPhone, with a 4.7 rating, Editors' Choice and a polished blocking experience. If all you want is a strong blocker, it's a good pick. ARCO is built for a different job: planning your day and protecting it, in one place.

Where Opal is stronger

  • Blocking depth: Allow Lists, long sessions (Opal mentions up to 14 days) and "always on" blocking.
  • Gamification: Focus Score, milestones and leaderboards, if that motivates you.
  • More platforms: Opal has a Mac app and an Android app.
  • A free tier: unlimited on-demand sessions and a few recurring ones without paying.

Where ARCO is different

  • A day planner, not just a blocker: tasks, habits and events sit on a timeline with a NOW line. Blocking is part of the plan, not a separate app.
  • Habits with timers and streaks, including focus habits like Deep work 2h that start a blocking session.
  • Wake-up alarms that ring like the Clock app's and open your day.
  • Counted breaks: a set number of short breaks per window, then the block comes back on its own.
  • One price for everything: after the free trial, one subscription unlocks every feature. No tiers.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Opal if you want the most powerful standalone blocker, use a Mac or Android too, or want a free tier to live on.

Pick ARCO if you're also trying to plan your days and keep habits, and want the blocking to follow that plan: shut during your deep work block, open at lunch, shut again at 22:30.
