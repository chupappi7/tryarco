How to block Instagram and TikTok without deleting them

Deleting apps works until you reinstall them. Here's how to keep them, but only when you choose.

By Thinh Tran, maker of · Updated 19 October 2026

Deleting Instagram and TikTok feels great for a day. Then you need to reply to a DM, reinstall "just for a minute", and you're back. Blocking works better: the apps stay, but they're closed when you've decided they should be.

Option 1: App Limits

  1. Go to Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit.
  2. Choose Instagram and TikTok (under Social), set the time to 1 minute, and tap Add.

After a minute a day, the app shows a limit screen. The weak spot is Ignore Limit, which gives you 15 more minutes, or the rest of the day, in one tap.

Option 2: Downtime with exceptions

Turn on Screen Time → Downtime for the hours you want, and make sure Instagram and TikTok are not in Always Allowed. Same weakness: Ignore Limit.

Option 3: Screen Time passcode

Ask someone you trust to set a Screen Time passcode you don't know. Then Ignore Limit needs their code. It works, but it means asking someone every time you need an exception.
