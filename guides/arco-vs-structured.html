ARCO vs Structured

Both put your day on a visual timeline. Structured is a beautiful planner across all your Apple devices. ARCO plans your day and also shuts the apps that would wreck it.

By Thinh Tran, maker of · Updated 20 October 2026

Structured is one of the most popular day planners on iPhone (4.8 stars, Editors' Choice). It shows your tasks and routines as a timeline you can drag around, and it has a generous free tier. ARCO is also a timeline planner, but it's built around one extra idea: a plan only works if your phone doesn't steal it.

Where Structured is stronger

  • Everywhere in Apple's world: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Vision Pro, plus Android and web.
  • Calendar and Reminders sync, and AI planning features on Pro.
  • A real free tier with the timeline, widgets and Live Activities.

Where ARCO is different

  • App blocking built in: Blocked Hours shut your picked apps on schedule, and focus sessions lock them until the timer ends. Structured doesn't block apps on its own (it can work with one sec).
  • Habits with streaks, counts and timers, living on the timeline.
  • Wake-up alarms that ring like the Clock app's and open your day.
  • Insights that show focus hours and screen time side by side.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Structured if planning is the whole job, you use a Mac, iPad or Watch daily, and your phone isn't what derails you.

Pick ARCO if your plans are fine at 8:00 and gone by 11:00 because of the feeds. Having the plan and the blocking in one place is the point.
