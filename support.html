Common questions

Blocking isn't working

Check three things:

  1. Screen Time access is on: iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → .
  2. You picked apps to block: in , Settings → Apps to block.
  3. Your subscription or trial is active. When it ends, locks and stops blocking.
How do I add a widget, or change which habit it shows?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for and add the one you like. To change what it shows, touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, then Habit (or Show on the Tasks widget) and pick. Step by step with pictures in the Widgets guide.

I'm stuck in Strict mode

That's Strict mode working: a strict session can't be ended before its timer runs out. Breaks still work (blocking pauses and comes back by itself), and every app that isn't on your block list works normally. It releases the moment the timer finishes.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged.

I was charged after my free trial

The 14-day trial turns into your chosen plan on day 14 unless it's cancelled before then. reminds you two days before, and shows it on Today. For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunds are Apple's decision, not ours.

My apps are still blocked after deleting

Rare, but easy to fix. Blocking is enforced by iOS itself, so a block that was running when was removed can outlive the app. Reinstall and open it once: it clears its own blocks on launch. Or go to iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → and turn access off. Neither costs you any data.

The screen time numbers look off

shows the last 7 days from iOS Screen Time, which can differ from Apple's Monday-to-Sunday week in Settings. Screen Time also counts background audio and picture-in-picture. If one app's number is wildly different from Settings → Screen Time for the same day, email us: that's a bug we want to fix.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud on for : your plans, habits and history sync over. Give Screen Time access once on the new phone, and tap Restore if it asks you to subscribe.
