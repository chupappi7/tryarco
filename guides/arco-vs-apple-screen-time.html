ARCO vs Apple Screen Time

Screen Time is free and already on your iPhone. ARCO is built on Apple's Screen Time framework too. Here's what the built-in does well, and where it falls short.

By Thinh Tran, maker of · Updated 20 October 2026

Every iPhone has Screen Time: it shows where your hours go and lets you set limits. On iOS 27, Apple reworked it into Time Allowances (daily time per category, like Social Media) and a Screen Time Schedule (windows like work or evening). On older versions, the same ideas are App Limits and Downtime.

ARCO is built on the same Apple framework. Your Screen Time data stays on your iPhone either way.

What Screen Time does well

  • It's free and built in, and syncs across your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Great reports: daily and weekly screen time, pickups and notifications.
  • Strong for families: with Family Sharing, a parent holds the passcode, and that's hard to get around.

Where it falls short for adults

  • "Ignore Limit" is one tap away unless you set a Screen Time passcode, and if you set it yourself, you know it.
  • Category-based: limits work best on whole categories, not just the two apps that actually get you.
  • No plan: it tells you what happened, but doesn't help you decide what the day should look like.
