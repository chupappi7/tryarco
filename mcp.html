Connect your agent

Pick your app, add ARCO, sign in with Apple once. Nothing to install.

claude mcp add -s user -t http arco https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp
  1. Run the command in a terminal. It adds ARCO to every project, not just this folder.
  2. Start Claude Code, run /mcp, pick arco and choose Authenticate. A browser tab opens: press Continue with Apple and sign in.
  3. Ask: "What's on my plate today?"
https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp
  1. In Claude (web or desktop), open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it ARCO, paste the URL, add it, then press Connect and sign in with Apple.
  3. In a chat, turn ARCO on from the tools menu and ask: "Plan my afternoon."
https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp
  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Apps. Under Advanced settings, turn on Developer mode.
  2. Choose Create app, name it ARCO, paste the URL, pick OAuth, then sign in with Apple.
  3. In a chat, add ARCO from the + menu and ask: "What's overdue?"
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "arco": { "url": "https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp" }
  }
}
  1. Open Cursor Settings → MCP and choose Add custom MCP, or paste this into ~/.cursor/mcp.json.
  2. ARCO shows Needs login: press it and sign in with Apple.
  3. Ask the agent: "Add 'fix the iPad paywall' for tomorrow at 10."
codex mcp add arco --url https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp
codex mcp login arco
  1. Run both commands in a terminal. The second opens the sign-in: press Continue with Apple.
  2. Start Codex. ARCO's tools are there in every project.
  3. Ask: "Check off the release notes and plan tomorrow."

The sign-in page says what ARCO will let the app do before Apple's sign-in opens. Use the Apple Account your iPhone uses for iCloud: that's where ARCO keeps your plan.

What to ask

Talk to it the way you'd talk to an assistant who can see your planner:

Morning

  • "What's on my plate today?"
  • "Plan my afternoon, hardest thing first, gym stays at 18:00."

During the day

  • "Proposal's sent, check it off and add a follow-up for Friday."
  • "Push everything I didn't finish to tomorrow."

While you work

  • "Add 'fix the iPad paywall' to the ARCO project, high priority."
  • "What's overdue?"

Habits

  • "I went for a run, check it off."
  • "Which habits are still open today?"

Tools

Thirteen tools. Days take today, tomorrow, a weekday name, or YYYY-MM-DD. Times are HH:MM, 24-hour; leaving a time out means Anytime. Priority is 0 none, 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high. Wherever a tool takes a task, it accepts the task's id or the exact title of an open task.

Other apps

Any app that supports remote MCP servers with sign-in works. Give it this URL; it finds the sign-in on its own:

https://mcp.tryarco.app/mcp

It speaks MCP over Streamable HTTP and signs in with OAuth 2.1, including dynamic client registration.

Privacy & sign-in

ARCO keeps your plan in your own iCloud, which is how it syncs between your devices. When your agent asks for something, ARCO's server fetches it from iCloud through Apple's CloudKit, the way iCloud.com does in a browser, and passes it on. Your tasks are never stored on ARCO's server.

Your agent can

  • See your tasks, habits and timeline
  • Plan your day and move tasks
  • Add, edit, check off and delete tasks
  • See your Blocked Hours

It can't

  • See your Apple password
  • See your Screen Time data, which stays on your iPhone
  • Change your blocked apps or your subscription

What ARCO's server keeps

  • Your iCloud sign-in for ARCO, encrypted, so your agent can reach your plan without asking you to sign in every time.
  • Your time zone, so "today" means your today.
  • Nothing else. Tasks pass through in memory and are never written down.
  • Gone after 30 days unused, or as soon as iCloud ends the sign-in. To disconnect sooner, remove ARCO in your app's connector settings.
  • Each app asks first. Every new app gets its own consent page, and the sign-in only works in the browser that started it.

Troubleshooting

"ARCO's iCloud sign-in has ended"
Reconnect ARCO in your app's connector settings (in Claude Code: /mcp → arco → Authenticate). That opens Apple's sign-in again.
"This iCloud account has no ARCO data yet"
Open ARCO on your iPhone with iCloud on for it, wait a minute for it to sync, then try again. Check you signed in with the same Apple Account your iPhone uses.
It says ARCO Premium is needed
Connecting an agent is part of Premium. Open ARCO 2.1.3 or later on your iPhone once so it can confirm your subscription, then connect again.
It says ARCO needs updating
Update ARCO from the App Store and open it once. That links your subscription to your iCloud so the sign-in can find it.
My agent doesn't show the ARCO tools
Finish the sign-in first, then start a new chat or restart the agent. In Claude and ChatGPT, turn ARCO on for the chat.
A change doesn't show on my iPhone
Changes usually arrive within seconds. Opening ARCO makes it sync straight away.
